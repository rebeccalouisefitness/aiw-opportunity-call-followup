How the next date is picked: the first call on this list that is at least 3 days away. A lead who isn't ready after 10 Nov is invited to 8 Dec, not to a call happening tomorrow.

Why the current lead shows "All caught up"

The app keeps one list of Opportunity Call dates. It only has 13 October on it. When someone registers, they're linked to the next call on that list. Anyone registering after 13 October gets no call (5 people so far, including the lead in her screenshot). With no call date, the Opportunity Call steps come back empty, and an empty list reads as "everything sent", so the screen says "All caught up". It's a missing date plus a wording bug. Nothing was actually sent.

The flow

  • Before the call: unchanged. Two automatic emails from AI Wellness (confirmation, 1 hour before), plus the coach's day-before and 10-minutes-before messages.
  • After the call: the coach taps Attended / Didn't attend. If they don't know, they can send the existing "Did you make it?" message.
  • Attended: same day thanks, day 2 their page (Customer or Business), day 5 check-in with a 1:1 offer. Yes → one tap to Customer or Business.
  • Didn't attend: same day replay + next date, day 3 "did you get to watch it?". Watched → joins the Attended steps from day 2.
  • Not ready / no reply: 3 days before the next call they're invited to it, then the usual reminders run again. After 3 calls with no reply they move to the monthly check-in.
